I spent two and a half years at Brandeis University, then transferred to UConn with my advisor, where I've been for almost two years. I've been lucky to intern at Ant Group, AWS, and Alibaba. I feel extremely lucky that my career aligns with my passion and that I get to work on something I truly love every day.
